# Barsmith Importer

## v1.0.0-beta1

First CurseForge release.

- `/barsmith`: paste a plan string from the [Barsmith page](https://rubensayshi.github.io/wowforever-barplanner/), **Check** what it'll change, **Apply** it. Creates or updates the plan's macros, fills the planned buttons and sets + saves the keybinds
- Spells you learn later (and items you pick up later) go onto their planned button if it's still empty. `/barsmith auto` turns that off
- Fills the main bar's stance and form pages, and copies them onto the pages EllesmereUI, Bartender4, Dominos and ElvUI switch to
- **Export my bars** (`/barsmith export`) turns your current bars into a string for the page
- Not tested on the Forever client yet (only on WoW Classic), so this is a beta
