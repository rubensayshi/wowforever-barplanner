Bar Planner

Set up your bars like you would in game: drag spells from the spellbook onto your bars, write macros, then use keybind mode. Copy the plan string at the bottom into /barplan in game (BarPlan addon) to apply it. Works with EllesmereUI. Your plans are saved in this browser, as many per class as you like.

Spellbook

Bars

Drag spells, macros and items onto the bars. Drag a button onto another to swap, or off the bars to empty it. Click a stance to plan the main bar for that stance. Click a button, then click spells in the spellbook to fill buttons one after another.

    How to use it

    1. Download BarPlan.zip and unzip it into the game's Interface/AddOns folder (where EllesmereUI is), so you get Interface/AddOns/BarPlan. Source: github.com/rubensayshi/wowforever-barplanner.
    2. Plan here, per class. The level slider greys out spells you won't have yet.
    3. In game, out of combat: /barplan, paste, Check, Apply. Spells you haven't learned are skipped.
    4. Later spells go onto their planned button when you learn them (if it's empty). /barplan apply runs the plan again.
    • Bars 2 to 8 use their own action slots. The main bar has extra pages that only show in a stance or form (slots 73–120), so a warrior plans the main bar once per stance. The arrows next to Bar 1 switch to its page 2 (Shift+2 in game); pages 3 to 6 are the same buttons as bars 4, 5, 3 and 2.
    • Apply only touches buttons and keys in the plan. A planned button ends up with exactly the key set here.
    • Spells, talents and racials are Forever's, from wowforevertalents.com (read from the Forever client and its hotfixes). New ones have a ★. Tooltips come from Wowhead's Forever database. Any name typed in by hand works too.
    • Turn on the bars you use in EllesmereUI's settings. The stance and pet bar contents come from the game; only their keys are planned.
